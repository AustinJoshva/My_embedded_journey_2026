# Components Used

* 1 × Arduino Uno
* 2 × Push Buttons
* 1 × LED
* 3 × 1KΩ Resistors
* 1 × Breadboard
* Jumper Wires

## Component Purpose

**Arduino Uno**
Used to read the two button inputs and control the LED.

**Push Buttons**
One button is used to turn the LED ON and the other is used to turn it OFF.

**LED**
Acts as the output and shows whether the system is ON or OFF.

**1KΩ Resistors**
Used to provide the required resistance for the buttons and LED circuit.

**Breadboard**
Used to build the circuit without soldering.

**Jumper Wires**
Used to connect the Arduino and the other components.
