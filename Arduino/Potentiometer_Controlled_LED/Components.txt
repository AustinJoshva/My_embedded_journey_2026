# Components Used

* 1 × Arduino Uno
* 1 × Potentiometer
* 1 × LED
* 1 × 1KΩ Resistor
* 1 × Breadboard
* Jumper Wires

## Component Purpose

**Arduino Uno**
Used to read the potentiometer value and control the brightness of the LED.

**Potentiometer**
Used as a variable resistor to provide a changing analog input to the Arduino.

**LED**
Used as the output. Its brightness changes depending on the potentiometer position.

**1KΩ Resistor**
Used to limit the current flowing through the LED and protect it.

**Breadboard**
Used to build the circuit without soldering.

**Jumper Wires**
Used to connect the components to the Arduino.
