# Components Required

* 1 × Arduino Uno
* 1 × LED
* 1 × 220Ω Resistor
* 1 × Breadboard
* Jumper Wires

## Component Purpose

**Arduino Uno**
Used to run the program and generate the PWM signal.

**LED**
Displays the fading effect by changing its brightness.

**220Ω Resistor**
Limits the current flowing through the LED to protect it from damage.

**Breadboard**
Provides a platform to build the circuit without soldering.

**Jumper Wires**
Used to connect the Arduino, LED, and resistor together.
