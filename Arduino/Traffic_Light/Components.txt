# Components Used

* 1 × Arduino Uno
* 1 × Red LED
* 1 × Yellow LED
* 1 × Green LED
* 3 × 1KΩ Resistors
* 1 × Breadboard
* Jumper Wires

## Component Purpose

**Arduino Uno**
Used to control the three LEDs and run the traffic light sequence.

**Red LED**
Represents the stop signal.

**Yellow LED**
Represents the warning signal.

**Green LED**
Represents the go signal.

**1KΩ Resistors**
Used with the LEDs to limit the current and protect them.

**Breadboard**
Used to build the circuit without soldering.

**Jumper Wires**
Used to connect the Arduino, LEDs, resistors, and power connections.
